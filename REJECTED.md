# REJECTED — do not re-propose or re-test these

Every row was measured against a random-entry control, day-clustered (z_paired), costs charged.
They are listed so nobody spends another day rediscovering them. **Before proposing any strategy,
check this table.** If your idea is here or a trivial variant of it, stop and say so.

Add a row here the moment a strategy is rejected. One line: idea, why it died, date.

## Inherited from the sister NSE project (already dead — do not re-run)

| Idea | Why it died |
|---|---|
| Participant-flow regime gates (FPI/DII/retail percentiles, divergence) | No gate beat the ungated baseline across 16 configs |
| MAX effect / lottery-preference (avoid high-max-return names) | Did not replicate; excluding high-MAX names hurt monotonically |
| Nominal share price / capital-gains-overhang as a filter | Higher return/trade but LOWER z_paired — they just cut trade count |
| 52-week-high nearness with oversold | Untestable — RSI<30 and near-52w-high essentially never co-occur |
| Price / CGO as candidate ranking inputs | Worse than a plain net-EV sort |
| Candidate-count gating ("only trade panic days") | ~half is market beta — random entries show the same gradient |
| Event-driven news trading (buy the filing, hold 1-3 days) | Negative in every regime; +0.00% edge even in the 2023-24 bull run |
| Short-term reversal as an extra filter on RSI<30 | It IS the RSI<30 signal — adds nothing |
| Shallow vs deep pullback within an uptrend | Opposite of hypothesis; artefact of what RSI<30 looks like |
| Raising a P(win)/meta floor to be more selective (0.50→0.55/0.60) | Net/trade rises but z_paired FALLS — fewer paired days; same trap as nominal price |
| Trading big single-day gainers (pre-signal tell or post-pop drift) | No pre-move tell; post-pop drift ≤ random. The move was intraday only |
| Illiquid small-caps (below ~₹25cr/day turnover) | Gross edge comparable, but ~3× costs make every band net-negative |
| Post-earnings-miss as a "skip it" safety filter | Bad-earnings oversold names do NOT underperform — the miss IS the overreaction reversion feeds on |
| Momentum-breakout / VCP swing (buy tight consolidation instead of dips) | No edge on 5y panel, every cap tier dead; the 0-100 confluence score has r≈0 with forward return |
